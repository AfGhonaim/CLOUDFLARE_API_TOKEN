Before you submit

Who can submit?

Abstracts can be submitted by authors who wish to present their work at Cairo FACE 2027. The Rising Star Competition is specifically open to residents, fellows, and early-career professionals under 35.

What can I submit?

You can submit work covering topics such as surgical techniques, non-surgical procedures and technologies, dermatology and skincare, and emerging trends in facial aesthetics.

What are the requirements?
  • Abstracts must be submitted through the Cairo FACE website using the submitting author's account.
  • Abstracts must be written in English.
  • Submissions should contain actual data and be well drafted.
  • Authors must fully disclose any conflict of interest and commercial support.
When can I submit?
  • Submission opens: 15 June 2026
  • Deadline: 1 November 2026
  • Late submissions: not accepted
What happens after I submit?

All abstracts are reviewed by the Scientific Committee and may be declined. Authors whose abstracts are accepted into the programme must register for the congress and pay the applicable registration fee.

Who owns my abstract?

You keep the copyright to your abstract. It may be reused, reproduced or presented again as an “encore abstract” at later congresses, provided the earlier publication is properly referenced.
