FAQ

Who can attend Cairo FACE?+

Plastic surgeons, dermatologists, aesthetic practitioners, and residents & young professionals — anyone building a career in facial aesthetics.

How much does registration cost?+

Early bird until 30 January 2027 — Egyptian: 6,000 EGP (instead of 8,000 EGP); non-Egyptian: $500 (instead of $700). The Pre-Summit day is 30,000 EGP for Egyptians and $1,000 for non-Egyptians.

What's included in my registration?+

Live surgeries, masterclasses, 100+ workshops, panels and discussions, and global networking with 150+ international faculty. The Pre-Summit advanced experience — 10+ live surgeries, hands-on courses, and advanced masterclasses — is booked separately.

Where is Cairo FACE taking place?+

At the Waldorf Astoria — Hilton Heliopolis in Cairo, Egypt, April 21–23, 2027.

What's the cancellation policy?+

A cancellation policy is available for all registrations — contact info@cairoface.com for the full terms.
