Explore the Cairo FACE 2027 agenda

Pre-Summit day

An immersive day in surgical and non-surgical aesthetics. Interactive live surgeries led by world-class surgeons — deep plane facelift, blepharoplasty and rhinoplasty — with guided explanation and real-time Q&A, alongside hands-on courses and masterclasses.

  • 10+ live surgeries
  • 10+ hands-on courses & masterclasses
