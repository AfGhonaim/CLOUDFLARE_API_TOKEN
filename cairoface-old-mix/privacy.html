Legal

Privacy policy

At Cairo FACE 2027, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This policy outlines how we collect, store, use, and safeguard your information, and explains your rights regarding the data you share with us.

01

Data collection

We may collect personal information from you directly, including but not limited to:

  • Name, contact details (email address, phone number), and professional details (Syndicate ID, Passport, etc.).
  • Payment and billing information for event registration are under the policies of Paymob.
  • Information provided during event-related activities, such as workshops or surveys.

How we collect data

  • Through online forms during event registration or course sign-ups.
  • Via direct communication, such as emails, phone calls, or on-site interactions.
  • Automatically through cookies or analytics when you visit our website (for website usage details only).
02

Data use

The information we collect is used to:

  • Process your event registration and course enrollments.
  • Provide you with updates about the event, schedules, and speakers.
  • Customize your event experience and improve our services.
  • Ensure secure payment processing and issue confirmations or receipts.

We do not use your data for purposes unrelated to Cairo FACE 2027 without your explicit consent.

03

Data sharing

We respect your privacy. Your personal data:

  • Will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • May be shared with trusted service providers and governmental departments (e.g., payment processors) solely to fulfill your requests and operate the event efficiently. These providers and governmental departments are bound by confidentiality agreements.
04

Data storage and security

  • Your data is stored securely in encrypted systems with restricted access to authorized personnel only.
  • We implement robust technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

Data retention

Your information will be retained only as long as necessary for the purposes stated or as required by applicable laws.

05

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Update or correct your information if it is inaccurate.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal or contractual obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for specific uses of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@cairoface.net.
