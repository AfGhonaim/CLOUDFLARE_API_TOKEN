Courses

Cadaver and hands-on courses

Twelve courses run alongside the summit — four on fresh cadaver and eight hands-on. Each is capped, led by a faculty member, and booked separately from the main program.

Reserve a place→

Cadaver courses

Fresh-cadaver dissection: anatomy you can see and follow a scalpel through, not a diagram.

Course 01

Structural preservation rhinoplasty

  • Advanced nasal anatomy focus: structural support, ligaments, and soft tissue dynamics
  • Structural preservation techniques with emphasis on function and aesthetics
  • Fresh cadaver dissection for true surgical experience
Course 02

Deep plane facelift

  • Immersive training to improve precision and confidence in deep plane facelifts
  • Advanced facial anatomy focus: ligaments, nerves, and vascular structures
Course 03

Cadaveric liquid facelift course

  • Risk management, complication prevention, patient safety, and professional medical photography (before & after results)
  • Injection strategies for volume restoration, contour enhancement, and facial rejuvenation
Course 04

Face inside out cadaver

  • Layer-by-layer full-face cadaver dissection with post-procedure verification of filler and thread placement
  • Ultrasound assessment and guidance for injectables and thread positioning
  • Hands-on practice with fillers, toxins, and thread lifting (selection, vectors, depth, and safe insertion techniques)

Hands-on courses

Live models and direct supervision — technique, product choice, depth and plane, practised rather than watched.

Course 05

The MAP masterclass

  • Advanced facial anatomy with clinical correlation for safe, precise injections
  • Capture powerful before & after results with professional medical photography
  • Master injection techniques with tips & tricks for full-face harmonization
Course 06

Profile balancing course

  • Select the right products, depth & treatment planes for predictable outcomes
  • Capture powerful before & after results with professional medical photography
  • Enhance projection & definition with advanced chin augmentation strategies
Course 07

No filler filler course

  • In-depth facial anatomy for safe, effective filler placement
  • Prevent & manage complications with anatomy-based approaches
  • Refine lip shape with symmetry & natural movement
Course 08

Non-surgical nose job course

  • Personalized training with direct supervision & tailored guidance
  • Live demonstration of the "Inverted Y rhinoplasty technique" on a model
  • Hands-on practice with one syringe of HA filler under expert supervision
Course 09

Advanced full face approach

  • Full facial assessment (upper, mid, lower thirds)
  • Combine fillers, toxins & treatments into one plan
Course 10

Ultrasound-guided injectables

  • Perform injections with real-time ultrasound guidance
  • Visualize filler placement & avoid vascular risks
Course 11

Russian lips masterclass

  • Master vertical lift, structure & definition techniques
  • Control projection & avoid migration
Course 12

Non surgical regenerative aesthetics

  • Deep understanding of regenerative medicine
  • Control projection & avoid migration

Places are limited

Course places are capped and sold separately from the summit pass. Registration opens with the main program.
