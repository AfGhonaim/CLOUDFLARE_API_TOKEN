Why travel and partner with us

Specialists don’t need a map of the world on the wall.

Visit Egypt Tours operates in one destination only. Two decades of inbound work in the same country means the ground knowledge, the operators and the fixers are ours rather than a third party’s — and that is what a reliable itinerary is actually built on.

The company works across leisure and corporate travel, from short city stays to special events, for travellers arriving in Egypt and for the trade partners who send them.
